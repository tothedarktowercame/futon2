## 1. E3: a supported pre-author stop

### The supported boundary

The existing restart debugger is sufficient for the requested *pre-author* manual comparison:

1. `futon2.aif.wm.debugger/attach!` and `arm-breakpoint!` are the supported state changes for the debugger (`src/futon2/aif/wm/debugger.clj:24-39`).  The repository's earlier click harness uses exactly this API (`holes/labs/wm-contract/runs/one-click-frontier-2026-10-22/attach.clj:1-4`).
2. Before issuing the click, arm `[:after :construction]`. `run-phase!` evaluates the construction thunk and then checks that breakpoint, attaching the computed result as `:debugger/result` (`src/futon2/aif/full_loop_runner.clj:400-416`). Construction itself is the `:construction` phase at `full_loop_runner.clj:7961-7969`; the first author contact is the later `:author-dispatch` phase at `full_loop_runner.clj:8317-8330`.
3. The stop is observable through `debugger/stopped`; its retained computed value is separately available through `debugger/stop-value` (`src/futon2/aif/wm/debugger.clj:88-109`). The serving click service also installs a watch and signals Agency dependencies `wm-debugger/stop` and `wm-debugger/stop/<run-id>` (`futon3c/src/futon3c/wm/runner_service.clj:1254-1291`, installed for each click at `runner_service.clj:1404-1423`).
4. Resume with `debugger/continue! run-id :continue disposition`, or abandon with `:abort`; these are supported choices (`src/futon2/aif/wm/debugger.clj:111-142`). The runner resumes/returns the retained result on continue and rethrows the discharged breakpoint on abort (`full_loop_runner.clj:442-493`). Abort therefore reaches the ordinary terminal close without entering `:author-dispatch`.

There is no WM-click preview, general dry-run, or `:stop-before` option in the ordinary runner/HTTP route. The supported mechanism is the after-phase debugger breakpoint. It guarantees **no author dispatch**, not a side-effect-free preview: outer selection, selected-target reading/redecision, construction, phase telemetry, and the outer-state pending write have already occurred. In particular, the missing-task path retains selection provenance and writes `:pending` before entering the task runner (`src/futon2/aif/full_loop_runtime.clj:648-680`). At the stop the operator can compare:

- the chosen target, cascade id/scores, Giraffe run and manifest, makeup digest, focus, and selected relation from the pending selection (`full_loop_runtime.clj:650-677`);
- the constructed mission/action value from `debugger/stop-value`;
- phase events, which are reported and appended by the serving phase sink (`futon3c/src/futon3c/wm/runner_service.clj:867-881`).

The operational sequence is therefore: attach and arm through the existing read/eval control port, issue the one authorized click, park on `wm-debugger/stop`, read `stopped`, `stop-value`, and the pinned pending selection, perform the manual comparison, then explicitly continue or abort. There is no dedicated debugger HTTP endpoint.
