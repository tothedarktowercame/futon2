## What the current controller actually computes

The current source path is verified: `full_loop_runner.clj:3738-3749` calls `wm/generate-war-machine`; that builds action scores with `efe/rank-actions` (`war_machine.clj:6435-6454`), filters executable entries (`6540`), calls `policy/select-action` with selection-law, temperature, habit/Fπ inputs (`6550-6562`), then passes the **actual returned decision** to `controller-authority/authorize` (`6572`). The authorizer checks exact admissible action membership, finite score, law and open mission/ticket status; it explicitly marks executed=false and leaves runner gates required (`controller_authority.clj:19-71`). The old fixture chooser is not that source call. Optional modes and exception fallback remain explicit in the source; no serving-env value is inferred from defaults.

Per action, `efe.clj:632-647` calls the forward model and uses predicted next observations (optional multihorizon final mean, first-step variance). Channel risk reads `pref/current-C` and KL preference densities (`687-702`; `preferences.clj:59-69`). Arena source defaults select KL risk and Gaussian differential-entropy ambiguity, with explicit environment alternatives (`war_machine.clj:784-811`). `:G-core` and `:G-efe` are risk+ambiguity, but selection's `:controller-score` also includes urgency/intrinsic risk control, predictability, survival, structural pressure when active, graph controls, posterior-spread bonus, gap bonus and goal outcomes, with optional other controls (`efe.clj:788-852,895-937`). `efe/rank-actions:983-1011` orders by controller-score.

This is **action-grain engineered prediction/scoring**, not an evaluation of the Lean cascade Model: the latter resolves every firing interpretation, picks the first applicable pattern, propagates probability kernels, and sums KL plus expected observation-kernel entropy across the horizon (`CascadeEFE.lean:58-89,402-414`). Gaussian entropy of a predicted channel is not by itself that conditional ambiguity; the controller augmentations are not additional terms of the Lean G. Sharing the printed name G or a risk+ambiguity decomposition does not establish conformance.

It demonstrates exactly why “no C is consumed” is wrong and why “the controller already computes cascade G” is also wrong. It does not establish the new source selector's recorded use.
