## What the rework adds beyond a translation table

1. **System 5 is where the operator-model work lives — and why
   stakeholders are System-5 phenomena.** A client firm's System 5 is
   its identity and preference structure; when the operator voices a
   client, he is voicing their System 5 through our blanket edge. The
   per-author counterpart slices (rebecca/eric) are per-client System-5
   models. This places Part III's business problems exactly: a business
   problem is a disturbance crossing a client's blanket that their
   System 4 failed to forecast and their System 1 can't absorb — and
   our modeling target is that firm's C, not merely our own.
2. **Beer's channel doctrine = typed records.** Beer insisted the
   channels between systems are the design, not the systems; our typed
   artifacts (constellation records, certificates, bells, ranked-actions)
   are those channels with precision rows attached.
3. **The AIF upgrade over Beer**: VSM is a DIAGNOSTIC (does the firm
   have these systems?); AIF makes it a DYNAMICS (what does each system
   believe, predict, and price?). The missing mathematics in Beer —
   variety as scalar bookkeeping — becomes precision-weighted expected
   free energy: G per arm, mission-parameterized. Beer's "absorber" and
   "amplifier" become priced channels with measured precisions.
4. **Fractality is shared and load-bearing on both sides**: VSM's
   recursion (every viable unit is a VSM) and our expansion-board
   recursion are the same claim — viability composes. A stakeholder
   firm modeled as a board of boards is not an analogy; it is the same
   data.
