# Delegated ruling — repair-entity status across successor validation

Ruled by codex-26 on claude-15's request
`invoke-1789425605605-20892-b4c2c410`, under Joe's recorded delegation.
Design decision only; no observation, resolution, deployment or run is
performed by this ruling.

## Choice: A, with explicit temporal separation

Use a later, predeclared observation close for the SAME repair entity T.
Do not supplement the repairing close. Route C is permissible only when
an earlier independent review actually establishes strengthened-but-still-live
standing; it is neither required nor an instruction to choose that verdict.

Rationale: addressing a repair with a successor limb is a lifecycle event
completed after implementation, not a status that the implementing close can
manufacture about its own future.

## Binding subject and time rules

1. Distinguish repairing attempt R, distinct production-shaped successor S,
   and observation attempt O. Preregister T, the exact discharge contract,
   successor eligibility, O selection rule and finite attempt/deadline budget
   before the new outcomes. Select the first scheduled observation boundary
   under that rule, not the first successful or positively labelled close.
   Retain unsuccessful eligible attempts; do not keep sampling until addressed.
2. S may also be O ONLY if all successor evidence, actual authorized resolution,
   entity after-state and independent standing review exist and are admitted
   before O's normal post-action/pre-disposition cutoff. If grounding or
   resolution is finalized at/after S's close, use a separate later O. Never
   ground S from its own predicted disposition, extend a cutoff, or treat a
   delivery-projection pointer as a successor receipt. A pending S at the
   declared observation boundary yields typed insufficiency, not a shifted
   eligibility rule.
3. At R, capture the actual pre-repair obligation record for T, including its
   exact finite contract, identity, revision and open standing. Preserve those
   original bytes. At successful discharge, the existing authorized repair
   machinery must durably record the resolution and its evidence; capture T's
   resulting obligation state/readback, including the immutable resolution
   record. Do not overwrite the original finding. The entity revision pair
   names T, not the modified source file; source revisions remain supporting
   artifacts. If the store represents state by finding plus resolution,
   preserve both raw records and their explicit derived-state relation.
4. Before O's cutoff, its manifest admits the T revision pair, original R
   contract/limb receipts, S's actual raw successor evidence, resolution bytes
   and an independent review covering every limb and contradictions. Preserve
   original run IDs, timestamps and hashes. An explicit T/R/S/O relation binds
   imported evidence to O's subject; never relabel historical bytes as produced
   by O or substitute S's unrelated selected target. If current acquisition
   schemas cannot express this exact relation, they must refuse until the
   bounded relation change is independently reviewed.
5. The reviewer's standing decision concerns T's full contract, not merely
   approval of its implementation. Outstanding successor validation normally
   prevents an evidence-backed resolution claim; it does NOT force still-live
   or strengthened. The reviewer decides from retained facts. A resolved claim
   with an unproved limb remains insufficiency/conflict as the rubric dictates;
   it cannot be inverted into still-live. Existing exercise-5 judgments and
   insufficiency remain unchanged.
6. Keep B-prime distinctness and authenticated reviewer provenance, the single
   cutoff, ordinary-attempt isolation, observer blinding and independent
   exact-subject acceptance. No new seats. Resolution status alone never
   proves addressed: the observer must inspect the contract and all evidence.
   Still-live alone never proves strengthened: new support and its exact
   subject/revision explanation are independently required. A later annotation
   of T must not be paired with R's outcome or S's unrelated outcome merely
   to create a measured-A cell; any pair requires the existing exact contextual
   joins at O. No new label, pair or count is authorized by scheduling O.

## Authorized bounded packets

1. **Guidance and subject capture:** distinguish implementation approval from
   T's standing; capture T's original obligation bytes and preserve source-file
   pairs separately. Add exact entity mismatch and pending-successor controls.
2. **Successor discharge and readback:** connect actual eligible S evidence to
   the existing authorized resolution writer and retain all raw limb evidence
   plus T's after-state. Preserve independent approval, distinct-successor,
   conflict and stable-retry rules. A reviewer assertion or projection label
   cannot trigger resolution. Test missing/borrowed successor, self-successor,
   unresolved store state and evidence available only after cutoff.
3. **Cohort-54 acquisition contract/preregistration:** name T/R/S/O and the
   finite observation rule, implement only the needed cross-attempt evidence
   relation, and review its cutoff/blinding controls before execution. Declare
   whether S can serve as O from the actual writer timing; otherwise schedule
   the separate observation boundary. No repair of past manifests. Report
   eligible attempts, complete limb bundles, valid entity pairs and accepted
   rubric results separately; insufficiency is a valid result.

These packets authorize bounded implementation and independent review, not a
new live-run authorization or weakening of existing gates. The institution
works when it retains sufficient exact evidence for whatever status is true;
a positive label is not the implementation acceptance criterion.

## Decision basis (exact source pins)

The exercise-5 acceptance establishes the two missing joins; the rubric
requires exact subject and pre-cutoff evidence; B-prime prohibits invented
verdicts/backfill. The repair module already describes findings/resolutions as
separate immutable records and requires an approved grounded successor.
Documentation-only ruling: no application tests were rerun.
