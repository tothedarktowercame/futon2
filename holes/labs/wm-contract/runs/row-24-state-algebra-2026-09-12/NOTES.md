# Row 24 certificate state algebra

This packet defines only the census vocabulary and parameterized candidate
acceptance predicates. `FullAttestation` is complete when its independently
declared node and connection universes exactly match the ordered state rows and
all seven record families occur in their declared order. Completeness does not
turn a refutation, absence, mechanism-only state, or unvalidated state into
support.

`qualifyingRun` cannot be invoked without a `QualifyingRuling`, whose selected
reading, ruled-out node set, and bytes pin are explicit data. The module creates
no such value and makes no choice among reading one (typed census as-is), two
(all nodes supported at this run), or three (positive except an exact ruled-out
set). Joe's §2C and negative-scope rulings therefore remain pending and are not
answered by this build.

No emitter, registry, fragment, certificate instance, or frozen subject was
changed.
