## 1. IDENTIFY

### Motivation

M-trip-journal installs the hinge-log / question-pool / sidecar apparatus
as a B→A tagging regime at mission-scale. The Shape A bridge (built
2026-04-18) now lifts that markdown into futon1a as typed entities and
relations — hinge-entries, questions, learning-updates, cross-mission
edges, all under `:source "hinge-log-bridge"`.

What M-trip-journal does *not* yet do is **mine the captured evidence**.
The hinge-log is a write-surface and the bridge is a lift-surface; both
are upstream of the learning. Running a single mission with a hinge-log
produces a saṅkhāra record. Running twenty-five missions and asking
*what capabilities is the operator developing across them* is a
different kind of question — it needs a mining pipeline, a capability
vocabulary, and a navigable condensation.

This mission owns that downstream work.

### The actual sorry

Twenty-five active missions is already past the threshold where a human
operator can hold the complete picture in head. The stack generates
landscape intelligence faster than a flat mission-ledger can surface
what's going on. The sorry: **there is no condensed, navigable
mental-model of the stack's mission-work as a whole, and no way to see
which capabilities the operator is actually developing across missions
as they run.**

M-trip-journal argues that landscape intelligence must meet operator
capacity or it's one-legged. This mission takes that argument seriously
at the cross-mission scale: if we claim the stack trains its operator,
we must be able to *see* which operator capabilities are accreting and
which are stalling. That visibility is what this mission is to produce.

### Two parallel scales, same move

The mining has a natural fractal — the same move at turn-scale inside a
mission and at mission-scale across missions:

| Scale | Base unit | B→A pair shape | Condensation target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Turn** (within one mission) | A single hinge | agent-view (B) → operator-turn (A), tagged with four channels × five skandha fields | A within-mission capability-trajectory navigable in WebArxana |
| **Mission** (across ~25 missions) | A whole mission | stack-state-before (B) → mission-outcome (A), tagged with capability signature | A 25-mission capability hypergraph that condenses the flat ledger into a navigable map |

Both scales share the extraction move (B/A splitting + tagging), the
tag vocabulary (four channels + skandha; subject to tuning), and the
render target (WebArxana as monitor; canvas as index over markdown).

### Scope in

- **Capability vocabulary discovery.** What are the ~8-12 capability
  dimensions that cover the stack's mission-work? Start small; grow
  by use per the four-channel learning loop discipline. The vocabulary
  is the mission's central bet — inventing it wrong is the main risk.
- **Turn-scale mining.** Extend the Shape A bridge to split each hinge
  into agent-view + operator-turn entities, tag each with four-channel
  and skandha dimensions. One full worked example on M-trip-journal
  before scaling.
- **Mission-scale mining.** Extract a capability-signature per mission
  from its mission doc (and hinge-log where present). Render as
  mission-vector in capability-space.
- **Navigable hypergraph output.** Populate futon1a with
  capability-entities, operator-turn-entities, and typed edges that
  connect them across missions. WebArxana serves as the monitor /
  canvas-index.
- **Agent-agnostic design.** The extraction and tagging pipeline must
  not depend on a specific agent; Codex, Claude, others should all
  produce B/A pairs the same way up to refactoring.

### Scope out

- **A→B pattern-activation meta-mining** (retrieval quality, pattern
  co-firing, temporal density). This is M-war-machine INSTANTIATE
  territory — it uses existing evidence-log data with no new tagging,
  and density-over-time is the first concrete visualizer demo there.
  M-learning-loop inherits lessons from it but doesn't own it.
- **Click/Tick machinery on top of the capability hypergraph.** That's
  M-hypergraph-operator's analytic layer. This mission populates; that
  mission reads.
- **Automated B→A extraction from live REPL turns** (QSR/QUR
  infrastructure per TN-halliday-clines). M-trip-journal's DR-2 defers
  this; re-entry condition is the hinge-log pattern proving itself
  across ≥3 missions. This mission works from hinge-logs, not live
  turn-streams.
- **Redesigning the hinge-log or question-pool format.** M-trip-journal
  owns those; this mission is a consumer.
- **A full taxonomy-ontology of operator capabilities.** The vocabulary
  is to grow by use with discipline, not be specified up-front. See
  M-trip-journal's "structurally-void sorry" discipline around the B→A
  shape.

### Completion criteria (provisional, to refine during MAP/DERIVE)

1. A capability vocabulary of ~8-12 dimensions, grounded in actual
   mission corpus evidence, is committed as a small set of flexiargs
   in `futon3/library/` (namespace TBD — candidate: `capability/`).
2. At least one mission (M-trip-journal) has its hinges split into
   agent-view + operator-turn entities with four-channel + skandha
   tagging, visible in WebArxana.
3. At least one aggregate view exists — e.g. a 25-mission
   capability-matrix — such that Joe reports the condensation
   illuminates rather than re-describes.
4. The navigable hypergraph shows at least one *non-obvious* cross-
   mission connection that Joe had not previously articulated.
5. The mining pipeline is idempotent and re-runnable as missions grow.
6. The B→A tagging scheme survives contact with at least one
   non-Claude agent session (agent-agnostic up to refactoring).

### Open questions (held — do not force)

- Does the four-channel scheme (pattern / vocabulary / connection /
  question) cover operator-capability work, or are there B→A moves
  that fit none of the four? Candidates for extension: refusal /
  preserved-void, inhabitation-signal, backwards-induction-output.
- At mission-scale, is the right unit *the whole mission* or
  *the mission × phase*? Mission-scale aggregation may wash out
  intra-mission capability-trajectory.
- How much of the condensation is automatic vs. operator-in-the-loop?
  The M-trip-journal I-1 discipline says operator writes hinge-logs;
  the mining pipeline can propose tags, but operator confirms.
- What falsifies the capability vocabulary? Criterion 4 (non-obvious
  connection) is one signal; others needed.
