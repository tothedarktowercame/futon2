## 1. IDENTIFY (shape-first)

### Motivation — the discrepancy

When `M-war-machine-first-outing` cleared the open-action queue, the only remaining recommendation was `:learn-action-class`. The WM was **starved of input sources, not done** (`project_war_machine_first_outing`; quantified in the expectations doc: 100% bootstrapping, 0% outward, queue exhausted into `:learn-action-class` before any `:open-mission` surfaced). An operator reasonably expects an active-inference agent to have "a built-in way to learn things." It does — but only *within* its model:

- **Within-model learning is present and honest:** belief update (R1/R3), structure/parameter learning via reliability posteriors (R7 adaptive precision; R12 dual-loop over inner hyperparameters). These pick among, and re-weight, the policies in the model's existing support.
- **What is absent:** any endogenous way to enlarge the *support itself* — to add a new typed-edge family / action class. Today that is an **external handoff**: `:learn-action-class` is a doorbell the operator answers. `niche-construction.flexiarg` states this as a scoped property of the current architecture (substrate authored by other parties), **not** a theorem about AIF — and the flexiarg was corrected 2026-05-31 to make that scope explicit.

The discrepancy: AIF theory has a built-in story for the meta-level (hierarchical priors that re-weight uncertainty-tolerance — Kiverstein & Miller; variational niche construction that redraws the blanket to include the constructed niche — Constant/Ramstead/Veissière/Friston). **Our implementation does not yet use it.** The gap is in the wiring, not the framework.

### The central distinction: backlog vs. installed priorities

Two ways to "feed the starved WM," and they are not the same mission:

- **(A) Big programmatic backlog.** Wire each of the five frontier sources as an enumerator-proposer (in the style of v0.18 `mission-registry` / v0.19 `pattern-registry`). The candidate set grows; the WM has more concrete `:open-mission` / `:fire-pattern` / `:address-sorry` targets to rank. This is **within-model** — R6 action-*proposal* scaling over a *fixed* set of action-classes. It produces a longer to-do list. It does **not** extend the support and it does **not** teach the WM what to value.

- **(B) Learn and install new priorities.** The WM *learns what to value* (intrinsic-value / preference-prior learning; a playful exploration prior) and can *install a new priority* — a new action-class, or a re-weighting that changes which kinds of work it seeks. This is the **boundary / meta-level** move: the support and the value-weighting become things the agent infers, not constants the operator sets.

**Joe (2026-05-31): (B) is the interesting target.** (A) is useful plumbing and may be a prerequisite, but it is not what `aif2` is *for*. The five sources below are therefore treated as **idea-stock for (B)** — raw material from which new priorities might be learned — not as a backlog whose completion would count as success. This is tension T4, and it is the mission's load-bearing commitment.

### Theoretical anchoring

- **AIF+ invariants** (`README-aif+.md` §3): **I1 Boundary integrity** (Markov blanket as a span) and **I3 Timescale separation** (slow constrains fast) are the relevant tier — both flagged as *sliding-scale, not mechanised*. The boundary tier is where "may the agent re-author its own niche?" lives.
- **The modeller inside the model** (`futon-aif-completeness.md` §"Capability-gap modeling as endogenous action"): already names the move — *"promoting model-upgrade triggers from external/implicit to internal/explicit first-class objects in the agent's state space."* Its three experiments (intrinsic-value learning; capability-expansion-as-discontinuity/morphogenesis; the agent's model of its own modeller) are `aif2` in embryo. This mission is **not greenfield** — it formalises experiments 2 + 3.
- **Niche construction, variational form:** the constructed niche is brought inside the Markov blanket; inventory-edits become variational moves over an extended boundary. The futon niche is symbolic (a typed graph), not physical, but the loop is the same.
- **Playfulness as the trigger mechanism:** a learned high-level prior that tolerates/seeks surprise reads a zero-anamnesis lull (a false-floor `ΔT=0`) not as "hand off" but as "forage at the competence edge." This is the missing drive that would turn `:learn-action-class` from doorbell into impulse — and it is mechanism (B), not (A).

### The shape, and its siblings

**Shape:** `endogenous-niche-construction-criterion` — a **boundary-tier** criterion governing *whether and how the WM may re-author its own typed-edge / action-class support, and learn what to value*. Distinct from the inference-tier R1–R12 (which all presuppose a fixed support). Candidate sibling instances (≥2 per shape-first discipline):

1. **`inventory-as-hidden-state`** — the action-class inventory itself becomes hidden state inferred by a slow loop. A genuine **extension of R12** (today R12 infers inner hyperparameters: intrinsic-value, EFE weights, preference priors — *not* the inventory) or a candidate **R13**. Checkable form: a slow-cadence process that proposes inventory additions and scores them by expected model-evidence gain.
2. **`boundary-redraw-admissibility`** — an **I1-tier** criterion: under what conditions may the modelled boundary expand to include a new typed-edge family? The **consent gate is the locus** (`project_consent_gate`): boundary-redraw is the first-class artifact; supervised→autonomous migration swaps operator for autopen at exactly this point. Checkable form: an `admissibility` predicate over proposed boundary-redraws (homolog of R6 `can-propose?`/`can-execute?` at the support level — cf. `feedback_admissibility_as_recursion_safety`).
3. **`playful-precision-prior`** — an **I3/R7-tier** learned prior that re-weights epistemic value so low-anamnesis states trigger foraging. This is the direct realisation of mechanism (B). Checkable form: precision/EFE-weighting on the exploration term that is itself *learned* (not the static credit in `action_proposer.clj`), with the false-floor lull as its input signal.

If only one instance ever turns out to be real, that absence of generalisation is itself a finding (record as `:special-case true`).
