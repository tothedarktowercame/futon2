## 1. IDENTIFY

### Motivation

Job applications, client outreach, pitches, RFPs, cold emails are all species
of the same genus: **expressions of interest**. The differentia that matters
most is the interest itself — not the format, not the credentials, not
fit-to-spec. The bottleneck on most outward-facing communication isn't writing
skill or targeting; it's **interest-articulation**, which is a separate craft
from either self-description ("here's what I am") or value-proposition
("here's what I'd do for you"). Both of those are downstream artefacts; the
upstream object is what actually pulls.

Under the August 2026 free-soloing constraint (no grants / VC / job
applications), every outward-facing communication is by definition an EoI in
the Hyperreal sense — interest in a piece of work, not interest in employment.
The discipline is: **never let a communication drift from "I am interested in
X" toward "I am available for Y."** The first invites collaboration; the second
invites assessment.

### The actual sorry (type-signature form)

There is a structurally-shaped gap in Joe's near-future trajectory. Multiple
basins of attraction (Anthropic-employee, postdoc, AI-startup, freelancer,
academic-affiliated, …) could in principle fill it, in *very different ways*
that route through nearly disjoint regions of phase space. INSTANTIATE-before-
MAP would mean choosing a basin because it is the legible default rather than
because its geometry best preserves the structural properties of the gap.

> **Sorry-EOI-PRIOR**: The prior document — what Joe is interested in, prior to
> any specific encounter — is not yet stable as a single live artefact, even
> though it is substantially distributed across the FUTON repos, the placemat,
> the sorry-factory thesis, the Baldwin-effect bridge, and the vision-since-age-10
> material. **Closure condition:** a short, durably maintained artefact whose
> shape is *capacity for interest* (structural conditions under which Joe
> becomes interested in a piece of work) rather than *content of interest*
> (a list of topics).
>
> **Sorry-EOI-PHASESPACE**: Even with the prior in hand, the basins of
> attraction have not been MAP-honestly enumerated against the prior's
> type-signature. Pre-condition: Sorry-EOI-PRIOR closed enough to compare
> bases against. **Closure condition:** an explicit phase-space document where
> each basin is characterised by what it satisfies and what it forecloses.
>
> **Sorry-EOI-INSTANTIATE-{vehicle}**: Each basin, conditional on having been
> chosen, demands its own brief — a 20-page-equivalent document analogous to
> the UKRN-S brief but addressed to the chosen vehicle's actual situation
> rather than to its possible recipients. Closure of any one is contingent on
> SORRY-EOI-PHASESPACE having actually compared, not on the vehicle being
> pre-decided.

The mission's job is to close these in order. Closing them out of order is the
named anti-pattern.

### Theoretical anchoring

- **EoI-as-IDENTIFY.** The unit of work is a Futonic mission's IDENTIFY
  phase, not a recipient-addressed document. Recipients are an implementation
  detail downstream of the gap being correctly identified.
- **Prior / likelihood split (Bayesian frame).** The prior is articulable
  before any encounter. The likelihood is the encounter-conditional update.
  The posterior is the actual co-articulated interest. Job applications fail
  because they ask for the likelihood from applicants who never wrote down
  the prior. Joe's situation is the inverse: prior unusually crisp, question
  is which encounters license which posteriors.
- **Capacity-for-interest, not content-of-interest.** The Julia analogy
  (interested in Julia, but only since meeting her) implies the prior isn't
  a list of topics. It is a description of the structural conditions under
  which interest co-articulates. This is harder to fake and more durable.
- **Eightfold Path / Seven Factors interweave.** The futons are Factors of
  Enlightenment (qualities that arise as the stack matures); the *interests*
  are Right Paths (ways of conducting oneself with respect to what is being
  built). Provisional bucketing:

  | Path | Interest-shape | Locus |
  |---|---|---|
  | Right View | invariance | futon3, the turtle-with-room-inside-itself |
  | Right Intention | morphogenesis | "things-that-are-built standing in the relation of things-that-grow to their substrate" |
  | Right Speech | flexiarg / de-ai-ify-ing — prose-as-debug-trace | AI-assisted writing |
  | Right Action | patterns-as-tangent-vector-field | agent interaction loop |
  | Right Livelihood | Hyperreal / August 2026 free-solo | five-Erics, Column-A/B, no grants/VC |
  | Right Effort | writing-into-systems-as-control mechanism | the vibe-coding-as-research-record contrarian thesis |
  | Right Mindfulness | self- and stack-self-representation; live model of self sufficient to act on self | futon0 sati/sampajañña |
  | Right Concentration | sorry-as-kasina; concentration on structured absences | M-a-sorry-enterprise |

  The mapping is load-bearing if it predicts gaps (a missing Path-shape =
  a missing way of conducting oneself with respect to the stack). Provisional
  diagnosis: Right Action is currently doing more work than Right Concentration
  in the felt practice; the Concentration → sorry-enterprise mapping is the
  rebalancing move.

- **Analytic frame (Jungian; Bion's "without memory or desire").** The
  practitioner's offering is a **held space** in which the analysand's
  material organises itself, with occasional well-timed challenges to glibness
  ("really?"). The challenge is non-adversarial, content-neutral, and rare —
  rarity is constitutive. The de-ai-ify-ing algorithm is the textual-scale instance;
  the sorry is the formal-systems instance; "really?" is the human-scale
  instance. **The legitimacy of the holding rests on the practitioner's own
  continuous self-analytic practice.** The daily frame-scans, the futon0
  sati/sampajañña, the live-blogging of AI collaboration are part of the
  prior, not preliminaries to it.

- **Pre-established harmony.** M-a-sorry-enterprise as a Leibniz-monadic
  enterprise: agents (human, Codex, Claude) don't communicate with each
  other; each acts on the local structure of the .lean (or analogous) file
  according to its own competence; coherence comes from shared submission to
  precisely-shaped typed gaps. EoIs participate in this discipline by being
  themselves typed-gap declarations rather than availability declarations.

### Scope in

- **Sorry-EOI-PRIOR closure.** Produce the short self-note (the IDENTIFY
  artefact proper) in *type-signature form* rather than narrative form.
  Articulates the gap such that MAP can compare basins without reverting to
  vibes-based comparison. Maintained as a live artefact, not rewritten linearly.
- **Sorry-EOI-PHASESPACE closure.** Enumerate the basins of attraction
  (provisionally: Anthropic-or-equivalent-frontier-AI-employee; postdoc /
  academic affiliation; AI-startup co-founder or early employee; Hyperreal
  freelancer; UKRN-S formal arrangement; hybrid combinations). Each basin
  characterised by its geometry — what it satisfies under the prior, what it
  forecloses, what it costs to maintain.
- **Adapted EoI-document template** (see § Template, below). A scaffold for
  per-vehicle EoI artefacts that resists drift from interest → availability,
  preserves the prior/likelihood seam, and gives well-timed-challenge moves
  ("really?") explicit slots.
- **One worked INSTANTIATE: the Hyperreal brief** (20-page-equivalent),
  as the structural twin of the UKRN-S brief but addressed to Hyperreal-as-
  currently-constituted (five Erics, VSAT, Brookes spillover, etc.).
  Conditional document — not a decision, an exploration of one basin.
- **Calibration via existing artefacts.** Re-read the Gary triage-protocol-
  ledger proposal and the Bristol "I'd like that to be a formal arrangement"
  sentence under the EoI lens. Diagnose which speech-act each is performing
  (interest vs. availability vs. prediction-plus-availability) and whether
  that's strategically right.

### Scope out

- **Recipient-targeted documents to specific employers / clients pre-MAP.**
  Premature INSTANTIATE. If the prior and phase-space haven't been done,
  any specific EoI is partially deciding the trajectory by the back door.
- **The 20-page Hyperreal brief written first.** Tempting because Hyperreal
  is the legible default; doing it first risks Hyperreal feeling inevitable
  by accident. Order: self-note → phase-space MAP → Hyperreal brief as one
  conditional document among several.
- **Mass-revision of the existing prior material.** The FUTON repos, the
  placemat, the sorry-factory writings, the Baldwin-bridge material *are*
  most of the prior already. The work is to *index* them as a live capacity-
  description, not to rewrite them.
- **Decision about the August 2026 transition itself.** This mission produces
  the artefacts that license a decision; it does not make the decision.
  Right Intention question (defaulting vs. choosing) is held until MAP is honest.
- **A taxonomy of EoI sub-genres pre-data.** Job-app, RFP, cold-email, pitch
  variants will surface naturally as INSTANTIATE candidates encounter the
  template; pre-specifying them is futon3-pattern-mining mistake at a different
  scale (cf. M-learning-loop's vocabulary discipline).

### Completion criteria

1. Sorry-EOI-PRIOR closed: a maintained self-note in type-signature form
   exists, and Joe reports that re-reading it before drafting any outward-
   facing communication actually constrains the drafting.
2. Sorry-EOI-PHASESPACE closed: the basins of attraction are enumerated with
   honest geometry (what each satisfies, what it forecloses), and at least
   one non-default basin reads as genuinely live rather than perfunctorily
   listed.
3. The adapted EoI template (§ Template) has been used to produce ≥2 EoI
   artefacts that pass Joe's "really?"-test on his own re-reading.
4. The Hyperreal brief exists as a 20-page-equivalent INSTANTIATE document,
   and its existence does not foreclose work on alternative basins.
5. The Gary proposal and the Bristol sentence have each been diagnosed under
   the EoI lens, with explicit speech-act attribution and (if needed) a
   re-articulation.
6. At least one previously-unnamed basin is articulated through the work —
   evidence that the mission is generative, not just descriptive.
