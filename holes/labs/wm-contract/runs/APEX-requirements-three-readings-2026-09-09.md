The useful question is what a requirement changes at a particular decision
boundary. A preference ranks predicted outcomes. An institutional rule assigns
rights, obligations and responses among participants. An executable pattern
supplies a situated procedure. These are proposed roles for this system, not a
proof of a mathematical duality or an assertion that all rules are enforced.
One requirement may need all three, with separate carriers and evidence.

## R-A — Records carry warrant

**Existing evidence.** Six inducing members concern derivations, completion
claims, trimmed returns, queryability, cascades and transport loss. The
first-flights replay distinguishes accuracy from warranted accuracy: removing
the measurement ground leaves numerical error unchanged but flips the
calibration mask. This is a concrete precedent for an admission check, not a
measurement of all six members. Source:
`futon2/holes/DESIGN-REQUIREMENTS-apex-2026-09-09.md:14`.

**Preference reading.** Define outcomes of a particular reporting action, such
as a claim arriving with a resolvable derivation versus arriving without one.
A versioned preference distribution could favour the former at report grain.
It would need an observation of receipt and derivation validity, a predictive
model of those outcomes, and warranted masses. None follows from the six-member
count. Its computational effect would be to rank reporting policies; it would
not prevent an unsupported claim being admitted as evidence. No masses are
proposed here. A preference over report quality is distinct from a rule that
only grounded reports qualify.

**Institution reading.** Proposed rule: the author supplies the derivation,
the reviewing role checks it, and a consumer cannot treat an unsupported
assertion as validated. Specify who may amend a ground and who resolves
conflicting grounds. Enforce at evidence admission and dependent publication;
retain a rejected claim with its refusal rather than erase the incident.
Record source revision, execution receipt, author, reviewer and admission
verdict. The behavioural difference is that a favourable score cannot license
an unsupported validation claim.

**Pattern reading.** Reuse authored apparatus procedures where applicable.
`futon3/library/apparatus/one-authority-per-question.flexiarg:15` concerns
facts duplicated across writers/readers; its THEN assigns authority and makes
copies explicit checked pins. For this requirement, a proposed interpretation
fires when a dependent report reads such a copy and obligates comparison to its
authority before publication. It is not a new authored relation from R-A to the
pattern. Control: change the authority without moving the copy; admission must
fail even if the report's numeric result is unchanged. A pattern interpreter's
execution receipt proves that check ran, not that institutional accountability
is complete.

**Recommendation for Joe.** Start with the admission obligation and executable
checking procedure. Add a preference only if choosing between admissible
reporting policies needs it. Decide which claim classes require independent
review; do not infer universal review from this proposal.

## R-B — One queryable self-account

**Existing evidence.** Four members concern incompatible evidence vocabularies,
provenance, observation lag and unused infrastructure. A capability-graph probe
computed prerequisite satisfaction for one completed subject; the unified issue
board is a working projection. Neither establishes complete, current coverage
of every participant. Source:
`futon2/holes/DESIGN-REQUIREMENTS-apex-2026-09-09.md:28`.

**Preference reading.** Candidate outcomes belong to a query at a named scope
and revision: supported answer, exposed disagreement, stale answer or missing
source. A preference could rank observation/repair policies by their predicted
ability to improve answers. It needs an explicit query domain and observations
of correctness/freshness. A single count of completed holes is insufficient:
exposing a previously hidden contradiction can increase the issue count while
improving the account. No scalar utility or preferred distribution is supplied
by the requirement alone.

**Institution reading.** Proposed rule: each fact class has a declared owner,
source and query interface; readers receive provenance, time and unresolved
conflicts. This need not mean one physical database. Enforce source declarations
at ingestion and expose missing/unreachable authorities in query responses;
do not silently choose a winner for conflicting sources. Assign responsibility
for repairing a broken projection, separately from authority to change a fact.
The runtime difference is an explicit incomplete/conflicted answer instead of
a seemingly authoritative total assembled from incompatible snapshots.

**Pattern reading.** The same authority pattern applies to replicated facts;
`futon3/library/apparatus/pin-moves-with-the-population.flexiarg:15` supplies
population/pin coordination. Its interpretation must preserve same-commit
population re-pins, distinct from same-slice source re-attestation; see the
reviewed correction in
`futon2/holes/labs/wm-contract/runs/E-pre-go-live-experiment-2026-09-09.md:1`.
Proposed trigger: a query population changes. THEN: update its governed pin at
the required mutation boundary and verify dependent projections. Control:
change membership while retaining the previous denominator; report a mismatch,
not a plausible percentage. This is a proposed application, not an additional
pattern or invented edge.

**Recommendation for Joe.** Treat queryability as a service obligation with
provenance-bearing projections and explicit incompleteness. Patterns govern
maintenance. Leave preference ranking of repair work separate. The scope of
“one” needs an explicit initial inventory; stack-wide completeness cannot be
inferred from the current board.

## R-C — Feedback reaches every participant

**Existing evidence and explicit zero.** Four members include operator turns,
human learning, trainers' equity and argument revision. No adequate channel-C
or seeded-C carrier is recorded for this requirement. The documented probe
scores “feedback delivered: false” like delivery because of substring matching:
`futon2/holes/E-C-realization.md:154`. This is a defect in the available
observation, not evidence of delivery and not a small positive preference.

**Preference reading.** A candidate domain is a participant-indexed feedback
outcome at an agreed checkpoint. Delivery, acknowledgment, understanding and
subsequent use are distinct observations; absent evidence is unknown, not
successful delivery or an automatic false. A preference over such outcomes
requires a justified observation model and a rule for whose outcomes count.
An average across recipients can hide the excluded participant, contrary to
“every.” Even a strong preference does not establish access rights. The
checkpoint/channel observation bridge remains open:
`futon2/holes/E-C-realization.md:74`. The constant kernel's finite risk
certificate cannot close that bridge or discriminate delivery policies.

**Institution reading.** Proposed rule: declare the affected participant set,
who owes which feedback to whom, permitted channels, opportunity to respond,
and the authorized response to missed delivery. Enforce at handoff/completion
admission where possible; otherwise monitor and record a violation plus its
responsible role. Evidence carries the denominator, individual receipt states,
time and exceptions with authority. A participant without an agent process
must not disappear from the set. This instantiates the information/participation
question at
`futon2/holes/labs/wm-contract/SESSION-C-institutional-constraints-2026-09-09.md:41`.
The runtime difference is a visible unmet obligation and an actionable recovery
step, rather than calling transmission completion “feedback achieved.”

**Pattern reading.** A prospective feedback procedure would fire when a result
has an affected recipient and no admissible receipt. THEN would deliver through
an authorized channel and observe the required acknowledgment, or produce an
explicit pending/refused result. This paragraph is a candidate interpretation,
not an authored flexiarg or commissioned outbound message. Apparatus
`futon3/library/apparatus/evidence-to-disposition-once.flexiarg:15` provides a
precedent for one disposition constructor and total consumers. Controls must
include explicit false, unknown, a missing participant and a disconnected
recipient; none may count as delivered. Understanding requires its own evidence,
not an inference from a transport acknowledgment.

**Recommendation for Joe.** Begin with the information/participation obligation
and repair the observation contract before giving it a preference. Joe must
settle the initial participant scope and required receipt level. This is the
strongest case for an institution reading; a numeric weight cannot supply the
missing observations or rights.

## R-D — Accountable next-action choice, including goal formation

**Existing evidence.** Five members concern the WM selector, policy-conditioned
EIG, policy-grain compliance, shared budget and pre-goal preferences. The live
cascade graph describes close-hole moves only. The task-local experiment was
selected by Joe; future task choice belongs to the outer loop and must emit
candidates, chosen task, evidence, rationale and authority constraints:
`futon2/holes/labs/wm-contract/runs/E-pre-go-live-experiment-2026-09-09.md:99`.
That requirement is not evidence that the outer-loop integration already runs.

**Preference reading.** At action grain, a declared C and predictive model can
rank outcomes of admissible policies. At goal-formation grain, choosing a new
objective needs its own domain, evidence and update/authorization boundary;
renaming a close-hole score does not provide these. A preference change must
record which module/version changed and how the next selector consumed it.
The current scalar risk contribution and outcome-indexed preference layers
remain separate types, as in
`mathlib4/DarkTower/WarMachine/PreferenceRiskSeparation.lean:1`.
The computational difference would be an explainable change of ranking under
a named preference/model; it would not authorize the action or prove the goal
was legitimate.

**Institution reading.** Proposed rule: declare who can propose goals, commit
resources, select actions, challenge selections and amend objectives. The
selector operates within those permissions, records rejected options and
reasons, and reports an empty admissible set as a conflict. Enforce at task
admission and actuation, with the decision record available to reviewers.
Budget arbitration and participation rights need explicit owners. This permits
accountability for the choice, including deliberate non-action, without
confusing a score maximum with institutional permission.

**Pattern reading.** Existing policy-grain rules actually execute through the
shared firing loop: `futon3/checks/playout_snatch.clj:200` and
`futon3/checks/find_organise.clj:405`. They can supply situated proposals and
precedence, with authored guards preserved. A proposed goal-revision procedure
would fire on specified evidence of mismatch and produce a justified proposal
for the authorized decision boundary, not silently rewrite C. Controls: make
an unauthorized action score highest; it must remain inadmissible. Disconnect
a learned update from the next selection and the consumption check must fail.
No cascade execution proves institution-grain force; that distinction is
explicit in
`futon2/holes/labs/wm-contract/SESSION-C-pattern-institution-duality-2026-09-09.md:100`.

**Recommendation for Joe.** Compose separately typed proposal, authorization,
selection and recorded consumption steps. Retain the hand-selected experiment
as today's input; do not turn it into the permanent outer loop. Start by naming
the goal-change authority and evidence that would trigger a proposal. This
note does not adopt a learning design or an adaptive-goal mechanism.
