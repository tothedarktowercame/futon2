# Independent review of `c8805944`

The corrections are accepted as design authority for the isolated store. A
digest object is published only from a synced temporary inode by an atomic
no-overwrite operation; overwrite rename is not equivalent. The owner retains
its OS lease while proposals are prepared. Stable retry bytes include the
caller-fixed commit timestamp. Genesis is a distinct generation-zero object,
and a genesis object without HEAD is interrupted initialization rather than
permission to initialize again. Finally, a locally valid chain cannot establish
freshness against rollback without independently retained expected-head
authority. This review does not accept production ownership or completeness.
