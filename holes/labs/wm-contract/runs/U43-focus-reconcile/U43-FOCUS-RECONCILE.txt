6. WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

   It mints NO clock edge, retries NO write, and changes NO focus.
   The chosen option is `record the divergence and let a later tick
   clear it` (:choices :focus-clock-reconciliation): it makes the
   dropped write VISIBLE, it does not repair it.
   record-selection-clock! is still a fire-and-forget future whose
   failure is printed to stderr and dropped; a tick that records
   :lagged-write-missing does not re-attempt the write. Repairing
   the write -- a retry, or a same-tick witnessed edge -- would
   change what a clock edge MEANS and needs its own witness rule
   named, which the row's statement puts out of scope. It remains
   open, and is NOT minted as a row here.

   The reconciliation is a terminal projection: computed from
   wm-decision and the previous trace record, both already final,
   and attached after ranking and selection. Nothing it records can
   move a rank, a weight, a temperature, an admissibility verdict or
   a selection. Default OFF; flipping it is J-gated.
