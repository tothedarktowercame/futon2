\section{Background: missions and flights}\label{sec:background-missions}

A \emph{mission} names a problem, owner, phases and exit criterion. Its
lifecycle is greenfield, scoped, active, complete. The mission-specification
tooling also checks input ports, output ports and typed wires, so an orphan
output or incompatible handoff can be a mechanical finding.

A \emph{flight} records a traversal of the War Machine loop: read, choose,
predict, act through a gate, measure and record. Missing judgements remain
typed unknowns; a record cannot supply a verdict merely because one is needed.
These instruments expose some failures in active work. They do not establish
that every completed mission's output remains in use, or that the paper's
account stays current between runs. The audit began in that gap between a
recorded completion and continuing operation.
