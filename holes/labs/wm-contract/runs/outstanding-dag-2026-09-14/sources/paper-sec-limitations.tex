\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Empirical scope: proof of life, not proof of work.}  This is the
limitation that governs the others, and it should be read before the results
are.  What the Field Desk ledger and the specimen trace establish is that the
loop \emph{runs}: that a scheduled click selects work against a declared model,
constructs a cascade, dispatches it, obtains an externally adjudicated verdict,
writes a typed witness the model did not manufacture, and carries its failures
forward as first-class records rather than discarding them.  Every number
reported here is a number about that machinery.  None of them is evidence that
the machinery \emph{helps} --- that a stack governed this way improves faster,
more reliably, or more cheaply than the same stack under a hand-ranked backlog
and the same underlying coding agents.  The preregistered experiment is the only
comparison in this paper with a control arm at all, and its headline result was
negative: the learned proposer did not beat the retrieval-prior incumbent on
success rate, winning only on proposal diversity.  We report it because a
preregistration that is abandoned when it disappoints is not a preregistration.

The missing study is not subtle, and naming it is more useful than approximating
it.  It requires a comparison arm in which the same agents work the same stack
without active-inference-governed selection, an outcome measure adjudicated by
someone with no stake in the harness, and enough attempts for the comparison to
mean anything --- which, at a label rate of dozens per day rather than millions
per hour, is the binding constraint rather than an implementation detail.  Until
that study exists, the honest status of the architecture is: a working,
inspectable, externally grounded control loop whose benefit is unmeasured.
Claims in this paper are scoped accordingly, and readers should treat any
sentence that appears to exceed that scope as an error rather than a hedge.

\paragraph{Theoretical scope.} The system is AIF-backed, not a pure Expected-Free-Energy minimizer.  Its canonical core computes $G(a)=\mathrm{risk}+\mathrm{ambiguity}$ under the declared outcome model.  The controller score adds the active engineering objectives identified in Box~\ref{tab:aif-retract}; the same table also records mechanisms outside value scoring, including policy-support restrictions and telemetry-only diagnostics.  For active controls, the retract explains the recognizable AIF role without turning the control into a variational quantity.  For telemetry-only rows, the table asserts only diagnostic correspondence and no causal AIF role in selection.  Production belief updating executes the categorical Bayes filter $q^- = Bq$, $q \propto A(o\mid\cdot)q^-$ under an explicit, normalised A, identity B, and uniform D, with likelihood mode and A/B/D hashes recorded in the trace.  This gives exact inference under the declared model, but A's entries are hand-set and the audited real corpus contains only one event type, so it cannot identify or validate the off-diagonal structure.  B is an explicit identity assumption rather than a learned dynamics model.  R3's green status consequently means model-relative conformance, not that A/B/D have been shown to be the best model of the operating environment.

\paragraph{Verification scope.} The inner-loop gates establish progressively stronger but typed claims. Replay pins establish provenance, arming, and score recomputability; CLean and Lean establish structural well-formedness; A3 build-match establishes that reviewed bindings are inhabited in the external substrate; and the operational witness establishes one closure behaviour --- a recorded discharge produces the specified ranking inversion.  The ordinary replay loader accepts deposits without CLean, whereas A3/build-match requires it, so formal structural verification is not a universal precondition of every replayed fold.  More importantly, none of these checks proves semantic adequacy, beneficial effect, or optimality. Those claims require independent adjudication of the realised outcome, and a winner-changing ablation or ranking inversion establishes causal activity only.
