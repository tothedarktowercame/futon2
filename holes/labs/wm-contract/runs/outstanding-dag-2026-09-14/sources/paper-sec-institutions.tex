\section{From stated requirements to governed commons: the workshop
transformation}\label{sec:institutions}

This section walks one instance of the paper's title question end to
end: a high-level problem framing is broken into design requirements, the
requirements are restated as patterns, and the patterns are interpreted
into Ostrom-style institutions---normative wiring that the same
infrastructure can carry as data. The instance is small (one live
standard repaired) but every step is real and on the record.

\subsection{The apex framing and its requirements}

The cascade-live view states its problem at the top, re-statable from
data
(\texttt{futon3c/holes/excursions/apex-thesis.json:1--66},
\texttt{status: in-force}): \emph{can a community of humans and machines
doing open-ended work leave an account of itself structured enough that
the work can steer itself---so that activity becomes inference rather
than motion?} The apex decomposes into four requirement clusters:
A, records carry warrant (``an assertion severed from its derivation
cannot be checked, learned from, or recovered''); B, one queryable
self-account; C, feedback reaches every participant; D, accountable
next-action choice. Independently, the view checks five live standards
against its substrate
(\texttt{futon3c/src/futon3c/logic/cascade\_real\_live.clj:242--259}):
regenerates, evidence, reconstitution, honest holes, composed.

The standards are design requirements in executable form---and on the
day of this writing one of them was false. \texttt{s4-honest-holes}
evaluated to \texttt{false} because the honest-holes dimension (O5)
held zero rows, while \texttt{s5-composed} was true: the composition
published with no hole record at all. An empty set is not evidence of
absence; it may simply be an unread dimension.

\subsection{Requirements as patterns (verbatim)}

The transformation pipeline begins by restating requirements as
\emph{flexiargs}---the library's pattern carrier, whose fields
(conclusion, context, IF/HOWEVER/THEN/BECAUSE, next-steps) will matter
in the next step. Two were minted from the framing above, in a new
\texttt{workshop/} family. They are reproduced verbatim so their
admission can be judged on their face.

\begin{footnotesize}
\begin{verbatim}
@flexiarg workshop/records-carry-warrant
@title Records Carry Warrant
@provenance apex-thesis cluster A ... restated as a workshop flexiarg
  by zai-7 with Joe, 2026-09-12 ... Not mined from transcripts; derived
  from the stated design requirement.

! conclusion:
  Every claim a crew member emits must travel with the derivation that
  warrants it — the query, the input digest, the witness — such that any
  other participant can re-derive or refute it from the record alone.

  + HOWEVER:
    ... In those cases the warrant is a STATED ASSUMPTION with a version
    stamp, never silence (the witness-status precedent).

  + BECAUSE:
    An assertion severed from its derivation cannot be checked, learned
    from, or recovered (cluster A's ground, verbatim) — and a crew whose
    members cannot check each other's claims has no separation of
    powers, only manners.
\end{verbatim}
\end{footnotesize}

\begin{footnotesize}
\begin{verbatim}
@flexiarg workshop/honest-holes-gate-composed-claims
@title Honest Holes Gate Composed Claims
@provenance The cascade-live standards s1-s5 ..., where s4-honest-holes
  = boolean(seq o5) is TODAY FALSE (O5 has zero rows) while s5-composed
  is true — the composition publishes with no honest-hole record.

! conclusion:
  A composed claim (a cascade, a page, a report) may not present itself
  as complete while its honest holes are unrecorded: every composition
  step that could have holes must either carry them or carry a typed
  statement that none were found.

  + THEN:
    Before publish: for each composed dimension, emit either the hole
    list or a typed none {:checked-dimensions [...] :holes-found 0};
    s4 becomes true only on typed evidence.

  + BECAUSE:
    ... Honesty about gaps is a precondition for the apex thesis: work
    steers itself only if it can see where it isn't.
\end{verbatim}
\end{footnotesize}

\subsection{Patterns as institutions: the interpretation is the work}

The library's patterns admit multiple interpretations, and the crew
design assigns each interpretation its consumer: the informal
Alexander-style reading feeds the map; a production-rule reading (guard
$\to$ action, chainable) is what the Workshop---the board-authoring
role---consumes to write wiring diagrams. The third reading is the one
this section exercises: a pattern read as a \emph{proto-institution},
combining it with preferences and deontic aspects
(\texttt{futon2/holes/labs/wm-contract/NOTE-pattern-interpretations.md}).

The transformation of \texttt{honest-holes-gate-composed-claims} onto
Ostrom's IAD rule types is nearly mechanical, which is the finding
(\texttt{futon2/holes/labs/wm-contract/EXPERIMENT-flexiarg-to-institution.md}):

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Boundary rules} $\leftarrow$ the pattern's \emph{context}
  and \emph{THEN}: a composition enters the regime when it aggregates
  more than one dimension into a publishable claim, and leaves it when
  each composed dimension carries either its hole list or a typed none.
  \emph{Circumstances of application}---which requirements bind which
  artifacts---are exactly the boundary rules.
\item \textbf{Choice rule} $\leftarrow$ \emph{IF/THEN}: the compositor
  may publish at any time; it must not publish with an untyped-absent
  hole dimension.
\item \textbf{Aggregation rule} $\leftarrow$ \emph{HOWEVER}: the census
  is scope-limited to the composition's own dimensions---a cost bound
  inside the norm, not an exception to it.
\item \textbf{Payoff rule} $\leftarrow$ \emph{BECAUSE}: fogless maps
  are debt the whole commons pays, not savings for the compositor.
\item \textbf{Monitoring} $\leftarrow$ \emph{next-steps} plus the
  register: graduated from a named counterexample carried on the
  pattern itself, to a register disagreement on the verifier's channel.
  The monitor is an existing crew role (the verifier), not a new
  institution---Ostrom's monitoring-by-participants.
\end{itemize}

The institution adds what the pattern lacked: positions (the pattern
says \emph{should}; the institution says \emph{the compositor must}),
graduated sanctions, and a named monitor. The deontic vocabulary is
already runtime-native: a must-not is a typed refusal on a wire, not an
absence of wiring.

\subsection{The first compliance act}

The experiment's first finding was live: \texttt{s4=false} meant the
substrate violated the information rule (the page complied by rendering
the falsity in-band). The repair followed the institution's own
prescription
(\texttt{futon3c/src/futon3c/logic/cascade\_real\_live.clj:242--259},
commit \texttt{d0741b04}): \texttt{fetch-edges} is loud on transport
failure, so an empty O5 at summary time already means the authoritative
query succeeded and found zero hole-target edges---a checked none, not
an unread dimension. The standard now flips on typed evidence, with the
evidence riding in-band as an \texttt{:s4-evidence} record (checked
dimension, holes-found count, basis, as-of).

Two honesty notes attach. First, the pattern's status field tracks the
repair's progress: until the running JVM reloads, the live page still
shows \texttt{s4=false}, which is now a stale rendering rather than an
untyped absence---the two-wire discipline applied to its own
counterexample. Second, the institution text itself remains a proposal
at Workshop precision; it was minted by no one but the operator. The
Workshop that authors boards and the Workshop that studies institutions
turn out to be the same room: in both, the rules are authored before
the run, and the participants---not an external regulator---monitor
them.
