\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The moves catalogued here were not designed and then implemented.  They were
extracted from a system while it was being built, broken and repaired, and
several carry the scar of the particular failure that forced them --- a
scheduler that fired into a sealed loop, an enactor that measured how faithfully
it had reproduced its own output, a reviewer whose claim to have reviewed was
taken at its word.  That is the sense in which they are offered as patterns
rather than as an architecture.  What we think generalises is not the system but
the invariants: record the decision together with the reasons that produced it;
keep the principled quantities separate from the engineering ones so a reader can
tell which moved a choice; never let a component certify its own output; require
a witness the system could not have manufactured before believing the world has
changed; and treat a failure as terrain to be crossed later rather than as
something to roll back.\worknote{The invariants listed here are now
partially machine-attested: 35 admitted witness claims bind named
quantities to Lean propositions at pinned records, and
``never let a component certify its own output'' is enforced to the point
that the R9 checker currently refuses its own admission pending an
external anchor. The certificate that would attest the whole set at once
is built to the honest-false stage only (rows 24--28).}

A pattern earns its name through repeated enactment and review, and one
implementation in one stack is one enactment; no entrywise promotion census
exists, so this paper makes no numerical or majority claim about which entries
have earned it.

The formal apparatus behind all of this --- the generative model, the scoring
quantities, the structure-learning operator, an audit of where the
implementation departs from the theory, and a preregistered experiment whose
headline result was negative --- is in the companion paper, which shares this
one's system and its specimen run.\worknote{RULED (2026-09-13, repeated from the opening): the companion is
futon-2026 --- formal and preregistered experimental material both.
RESOLVED (2026-09-14): the Methods section (\S\ref{sec:methods}) carries
the Lean certificate.}

For the pattern reader, the test we cannot run is the one worth running.  If
these invariants are real, they should be recognisable in harnesses built for
entirely different reasons by people who have never read this paper.  Where they
are, the entry has earned its promotion; where they are not, it was a description
of our system all along, and we would rather find that out than not.
