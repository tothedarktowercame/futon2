The recorded factoring map presents \FactoringEdges{} mechanisms as
steps in a single attempt. The factoring asks which constraints govern them
across attempts; several mechanism entries state their constraint inline.

Two numbering schemes meet here and they name different kinds of thing. The
factoring registry retains its implementation \emph{mechanism} identifiers; this paper numbers the governing \emph{constraints}
recovered from the bulletin record \texttt{WR-0}, \texttt{WR-4}, and so on. A
mechanism is a step somebody built; a ruling is a constraint that step has to
satisfy.

Mechanism \Rpp{17}, the separation of whatever proposes a merge from whatever
accepts it, is the clearest instance --- its own text carries ``Invariant to preserve: whatever proposes the
merges must not also be what accepts them,'' which is the separation of
proposer from acceptor that \texttt{WR-25} rules on generally.

The factoring below therefore \emph{recovers} structure rather than imposing
it. \FactoringEdges{} mechanisms map onto \FactoringWRCovered{} constraints;
\FactoringUnmapped{} do not map, for three different reasons the table
records, of which one --- the preference organ terminates at an operator who
is not in the model --- is structural and is the subject of
\S\ref{sec:operator}.

\paragraph{A note on where the catalogue ends.} Of the \FactoringEdges{}
mechanisms factored here, the historical factoring scheme names \wmR{18}, which is not a
canonical assignment in the current control-map registry. Here it is the \emph{faithfulness audit}: it classifies every named quantity in
the loop as derived from the free-energy principle, a principled approximation,
analogical --- ``a heuristic wearing the name'' --- or an engineering control
that makes no theoretical claim, and attaches to each a plain-language
truth-in-labelling of the form \emph{claims to be\ldots{} / actually
computes\ldots{}}. The small dots along the top edge of every node in
Figure~\ref{fig:wr-overlay} are its output. This historical identifier must not be joined to the current catalogue by
number alone. The issue board retains that numbering distinction.
