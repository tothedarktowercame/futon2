=== 7. WHAT IS LEFT TO CHOOSE ===

  MEASURED, not argued:
   - All three arms fix what U12 named: the double 0.0 no longer reads as met, and
     nil is a typed absence rather than a satisfied criterion (§1, §5).
   - None of them changes clause (b). risk_mis is one number copied to every
     mission action; an outcome reading cannot make it vary (§4).
   - None of them reaches the clause (c) zero. That is the T=0.1 floor (§5).
   - ONE OF U12's TWO FINDINGS IS LIFTED, and it is worth separating from the
     choice below. U12: 'risk_mis does not vary with the world either -- 1 distinct
     value over 14 channels'. Under :numeric-equality the same sweep returns 2
     distinct values (§2), so the number moves with the world once the type
     comparison is fixed. That is a property of the outcome reading and it holds
     for every arm that scores anything at all; it is not the residual.
   - THE COST IS COVERAGE, and it is measured in §2. Over the 14 channels R2
     observes, :numeric-equality scores every one, :declared-binarization scores
     only the channels reading exactly 0 or 1, and :typed-binary-only scores none,
     because no criterion in the corpus declares an observable kind.
   - :declared-binarization and :typed-binary-only are INDISTINGUISHABLE on this
     corpus at the composition (§2, §4, §5) and part company only where a
     declaration exists (§1, §6, and the two declared columns).

  THE RESIDUAL CHOICE, stated as what the numbers do NOT settle: whether a
  criterion whose observable carries no declaration should be SCORED on a type
  fix alone (:numeric-equality -- 14/14 channels, and 0.75 reads as met) or
  REFUSED until someone writes the declaration down (:declared-binarization,
  :typed-binary-only -- 4/14 and 0/14). Both are self-consistent; the measurement
  says what each costs and cannot say which cost is the right one to pay,
  because nothing recorded says whether R2's channels ARE binary observables.
  That is a question about what the criteria mean, not about the code.
