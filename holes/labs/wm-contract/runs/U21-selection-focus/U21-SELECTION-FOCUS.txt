4. WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

   The flag is default OFF and the flag-off judgement is byte-identical:
   tick-mission-focus returns the durable read unchanged, and
   carry-mission-focus adds no field. Flipping the default is J-gated.
   The focus is derived from wm-decision, which is final where it is
   bound, and is read only by mission-c-readback and the projection --
   both after ranking and selection, so nothing it changes can move a
   rank, a weight, a temperature, an admissibility verdict or a choice.
   It mints NO clock edge: a clock edge is witnessed evidence and this
   is a projection of the tick's own decision, which is why
   :active-mission keeps its meaning and :mission-focus is separate.
