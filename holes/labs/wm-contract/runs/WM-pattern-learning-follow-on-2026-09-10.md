The proposed WM cycle is:
verified task artifact -> reviewed example and teachable decomposition ->
independent learner application on another task -> localized error evidence ->
reviewed pattern/index/example revision -> observed next learner attempt.

Requirements to carry into a future design:

1. Retain goals, pattern revisions, applicability conditions, intermediate
   constructions and gaps alongside the verified product. Execution success
   alone does not establish a reusable pattern.
2. Keep task decomposition, authored library relations and example/memory
   links distinct. A reconstructed cascade is a sourced interpretation.
3. Record discovery, actual exposure, premise checks, attempted use, feedback
   and verified outcome separately. The learner attempts the target before
   reference-solution exposure; disclose any unenforced access boundary.
4. Distinguish retrieval failure, applicability recognition, missing conditions,
   construction difficulty, library defects and verification failure. Changes
   must address the evidenced cause; citations alone earn no success credit.
5. Missing methods can motivate drafts. Promotion requires cross-example
   comparison, explicit failure conditions and independent review. A new pattern
   cannot certify the obligation that motivated its authoring.
6. Retrospective work and live teaching feed the same versioned library, keeping
   statement variants, conditional successes and failed transfers visible.
7. A repaired artifact must be consumed in a subsequent learner attempt. Retain
   before/after exposure and products; explanation quality or coverage counts
   are hypotheses, not evidence of improved learning.

This extends the existing learning-design requirement to name each learned
object, evidence, update, schedule, persistent state and next consumer. It does
not replace parameter-learning contracts or settle their pending choices.
Pattern-library revision and outer-loop task selection are different operations.
The pilot may inform both, but no autonomous learning controller or causal
benefit is established by its commission.
