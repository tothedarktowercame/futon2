## 4. The grain gate is not an equation, and why

`grain_gate/grain-gate` (`src/futon2/aif/grain_gate.clj:65-127`, box
`:r5-grain-gate`) **defines no symbol that any equation imports.** Its whole
output is a typed refusal — `:grain-not-declared`, `:grain-mismatch`,
`:scope-mismatch`, `:resolver-missing`, `:arglist-mismatch`, `:sha-mismatch`,
first refusal winning — or a pass. It compares the grain a cascade CHOSE
against the grain a planned attempt DECLARES, and checks the declared resolver
exists with the recorded arglist in the file at the recorded sha256.

Under TN §1 standard 1 ("an edge is justified by an equation or it is
plumbing") that makes it **plumbing**: an endpoint that runs no equation.

The sharper statement, because "plumbing" undersells what it does: the gate
restricts **which enactments are admissible**, which is the same shape Clause T
gives feasibility — "feasibility acting as policy support (membership in `T_f`)
and not as a term of G". Neither is a term; both restrict the support the
selection ranges over. If the owner wants support restriction visible in Figure
6A at all, it is one concept with two instances (feasibility at target grain,
grain gate at enactment grain), and it needs a drawn box (§0a) — it is not
something this delta can add.

`:plumbing` in the registry is a list of NODES, not of functions, so **no
registry row is proposed for the grain gate.** Recorded so the absence is a
decision and not an oversight.
