**Defect 3 — the fixture polarities are followed everywhere and named nowhere.** All four modules have an accepting, a refusing-broken, and a refusing-plausible-fix witness. I could assign them by reading, but nothing in the build could. A module missing its plausible-fix witness is the failure that matters — that's the one stopping the naive repair — and nothing would have noticed.

**On "holistic": I'd resist the obvious move.** A top-level `warMachineCompliant` conjoining every module would be false unification — `CoverageReport` is parametric in an outcome type, `PolicyGrade` quantifies over finished runs, `CommitmentTemperature` over selectors, `GainChain` over fold occurrences. There's no shared carrier, so the conjunction would be a tuple wearing the word "sound". The object that legitimately holds them together is the one APM already uses: **the emitted contract document**. Holism belongs there, and it's currently missing half the modules.

So the standard is six clauses (one vocabulary; three polarities named; every field constrained; in the contract or explicitly out; axioms declared; a Clojure mirror or a typed hole) — five of which are already true of at least one module, so it's mostly making practice checkable.
