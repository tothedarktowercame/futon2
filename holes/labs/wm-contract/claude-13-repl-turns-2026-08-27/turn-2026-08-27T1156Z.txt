The survey is at `ca97b84`, and it **corrected my own plan**, which is the useful outcome.

**The headline: it's two directions, not one.**

| direction | who emits | who judges |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Lean emits the contract JSON | Clojure validates the *document* |
| **2** | Clojure emits the trace | **Lean judges the run** |

`APMCampaignTraceChecker.lean`'s docstring is the sentence to take over verbatim: *"The Clojure side emits **observations, not verdicts**. This checker parses that artifact and accepts exactly a complete refinement of the Lean-owned phase order."* Verdicts about runs live in Lean, where they can carry theorems.

**And the mechanism is far smaller than I expected.** Both Lean sides are just `lake env lean --run`. The emitter's `main` prints compressed JSON to stdout; the checker's `main : List String → IO UInt32` takes a path and **the exit code is the verdict**. No binaries, no FFI, no build product beyond `.olean`.

**The subtlety worth copying:** `generated_contract.clj` doesn't merely obey the JSON. It carries its own `required-bounds`, `required-dispatch-policy`, `required-receipt-schemas`… and validates the emitted contract *against* them, so drift in **either** direction fails. That isn't a contradiction of *"the runtime is not permitted to carry an independently maintained phase table"*, because two categories are handled differently — **structure is derived** (`expected-transitions` is computed from the emitted `phase-order`, never restated) and **values are pinned on both sides**. A Clojure that only obeys can't detect a drifted Lean; one that only declares *is* the forbidden table.

**So H3 was wrong as I wrote it.** It fused both directions and put run-verdicts in Clojure. Corrected at `64ded76`:

- **H3a** — `generated_wm_contract.clj` validates the contract *document* against pinned expectations.
- **H3b** — `WarMachineTraceChecker.lean`, verdict as exit code, and **this is where the 2026-07-09 retro-trip belongs**, because it's a verdict about a run.
