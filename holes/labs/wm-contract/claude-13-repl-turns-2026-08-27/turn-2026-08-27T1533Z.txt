**But I don't think the meta-theory is a new construction. It's R5 applied to itself:**

| R5, over outcomes | the meta-theory, over clauses |
|---|---|
| the criterion set declares its coverage | the contract declares which clauses are checkable against available traces |
| an outcome outside coverage is `uncovered`, never absent | a clause that can't be witnessed is `unwitnessable`, never silently passed |
| `∃ outcome, covers o = false` — it names a boundary | `∃ clause, ¬checkable` — a contract claiming total coverage is the failure mode |

`CoverageReport.lean` is already parametric in the outcome type, so **instantiating `Outcome := Clause` gives the meta-theory for free** — which is what "the same requirement one level up" ought to mean if the vocabulary is worth anything.

And APM has it partly built already: `qualification-report-v1.edn` carries `non-vacuity`, per-category mutation coverage, and **residual hole tests** — it records the unexercised part as *holes* rather than silence. That exists in the precedent and not in ours.

**So the packet now requires the check to report its own coverage.** Per clause: `:passed`, `:failed`, or **`:unwitnessable` with the missing field named** — a first-class result, not an error and not a skip. A run where every clause is unwitnessable must say exactly that.
