### Three guards credited coverage they hadn't earned

Same family as the `(contains? p1-actions :abstain)` defect, one level subtler each time — not true in every round, but true in every round where they were evaluated.

1. **`accept-an-offer-that-beats-holding` is a P2 pattern given a P1-shaped guard**, byte-identical to `ask-for-surplus-not-surrender`'s. P2 here is a fixed disposition, so the harness was crediting coverage to a decision it never makes. Now `:actor :p2`, excluded from P1's set. Making P2 pattern-driven would let it back in — that's still the right next packet.
2. **`:offer-size` and `:ask-size` were both `(if (= act :offer) 1 0)`**, so `(pos? …)` on either *is* `:offer-made?`. The game genuinely names a give and an ask separately; this policy doesn't, so a pattern about ask *size* can't be tested here. Guard reduced to the honest condition, limitation written down.
3. **`:counterpart-tokens` was initialised to 10 and never updated** — a constant dressed as state, so its `(pos? …)` could not fail. Dropped.

I added `(show :g2 :snatcher 12)` and `(show :g5 :sharer)`.
