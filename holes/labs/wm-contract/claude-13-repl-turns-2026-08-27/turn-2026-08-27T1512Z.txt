**The design rule that fell out, and I think it's the general one:**

> **Some outcome in the space must carry zero predicted mass** — otherwise no outcome could refute the prediction. That's the mirror discipline in distribution form.

S-001 has two such outcomes of four. A Q that spreads mass everywhere is the stochastic version of a witness that cannot fail — and it's exactly the trap a fitted distribution falls into, which is why the 82 flights couldn't be retrofitted.
