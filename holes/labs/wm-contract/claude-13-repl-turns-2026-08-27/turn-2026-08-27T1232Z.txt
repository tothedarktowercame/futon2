**codex-22 was right to stop, and the blocker was mine.** My packet forbade `lake build` — meant to protect `.lake/packages` after this morning's repair — on a file that *imports* `GainChain`, and an import needs an `.olean`. It refused to work around it with an alternate `LEAN_PATH` or a source-adjacent `.olean`, which is exactly right. I built `DarkTower.WarMachine.GainChain` (2 jobs, seconds, `.lake/packages` untouched) — which is precisely what APM's own `regenerate-apm-contract.sh` does before running its emitter.

**Then a real review finding: the `holds` field had to go.** codex-22's instinct was the one I'd asked for — derive, don't retype — and it wrote *"`decide` makes status an evaluation of the imported predicates, rather than copying the current false statuses into the JSON."* But it fails twice over:

- **Technically** — the predicates are existential `Prop`s, not `Decidable`, so `decide` doesn't typecheck. That was the error.
- **Conceptually, and worse** — `identityThreadingHolds := decide (threadedIdentity clockSplitOccurrence clockSplitSelection)` evaluates the **two-clocks counterexample**, which fails family 1 *by construction*. The field would have reported a property of the fixture, not of the War Machine.

The principled fix is the one my own APM survey already implies: **a contract states what must be true; whether it *was* true on a run is a verdict, and verdicts belong to the trace checker.** I put `"holds": false` in the packet's suggested shape and reintroduced exactly the direction-1/direction-2 conflation I'd corrected in H3 two turns earlier. Statuses live in the rosetta and the qualification record instead.
