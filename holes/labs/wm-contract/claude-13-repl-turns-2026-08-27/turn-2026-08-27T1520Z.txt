It's a genuine held-out relatedness signal, independent of the directive layer — which is exactly what makes it usable. **But two constraints, and the second is a trap.**

**It validates `@how`/`@see-also` and cannot validate `@why`.** §5a's own corollary: *"Missions record the how; the why lives in a register the mission format has no slot for."* Co-citation is evidence of method-relatedness, not authority. **A spider scored on this corpus is being scored on half its output.**

**And the top co-citations are boilerplate.** Four patterns — `futon-theory/futonic-logic`, `invariant-coherence/drain-channel-shape`, `structure/block-as-futonic-revolution`, `structure/cook-ting` — are co-cited **exactly 14 times each, pairwise, all six pairs**. That's one repeated block across fourteen missions, not fourteen independent judgements. A spider validated against that **learns the template**. The corpus needs de-duplicating by mission boilerplate before it's ground truth at all — otherwise the highest-scoring "edges" carry the least information, which is I1 arriving in the *evaluation set* rather than in the vocabulary.
