as a missions-only experiment after the conditions below are evidenced.
Maximum: one admitted click, charged once under existing budget semantics;
no second ordinary issuance or automatic campaign chaining under this disposition.

exception expires then and returns unresolved deficits to HOLD.

automatic repeat avoidance, actual successor learning, or the whole loop.

prediction, effects and first divergence must be retained independently of
summary success flags. Do not spend a click merely to rediscover a known fault.

## What holds, and what does not

B1-TICKETS does not hold this explicitly missions-only experiment; it still
holds any whole-population or ticket-capability claim. B4 requires reviewed
activation before the attempt and live evidence to close. E2 actual consumption
cannot be closed by the first click alone; typed deferral is truthful reporting,
not learning. A later successor needs its own decision and authorization.

E3-OUTER-REPEAT/E3-T12 hold issuance if the pre-dispatch manual safeguard is
unavailable. R28 holds it if failed-reading evidence is unreliable. Any A1–A4
failure holds its relevant admission/dispatch boundary. Adoption plus the two
packets alone is therefore not sufficient clearance.
