The theorem, in one paragraph

PROOF-2a says the War Machine completes a mission on its own: a flight of clicks on one target ends in closure, where the machine chose the target (Clause T), constructed an ordinary field of candidate cascades from the mission's text (Clause 0), enacted the chosen candidate (Clause C), and the enactment met the mission's own completion criteria (Clauses 1–6). Its hypothesis needs one witness, a mission completed with library-warranted steps. Every stop or absence on the way is a hole.

The machine’s Cτ

Two families are live, and different scorers consume them (CTAU-D, futon3c e30a3a54; registry futon2 cf6aca6a). The token family is built by preference-member over subsets of a problem’s token universe from the cascade spec’s wants and evidence with λ and μ, and horizon-g-sparse* takes risk against it at each step τ; it is consumed by the per-problem lane’s R5 step and by the constructor’s :evaluate-g, and by nothing else. The class family is the literal {focused .55, related .35, unrelated .05, stop-the-line .05} at τ = T, with unit mass on :ending/not-yet-evaluated below it so risk is exactly zero before the horizon; it is what the tick’s joint decision scores, and its scorer says in its own comment that classes are not routed through preference-member.

The placement is per target, and no run record says which one ran. A target with a declared source file gets :terminal — four of the five sources declare it — and a target with no source entry gets :every-step, defaulted. That is a reading of the code: the run record carries no :c-schedule, so no flight record states the placement it ran under.

Hop 1 is not wired. Mission text → extracted outcomes → the cascade spec does not exist on the live path. The wants that reach the spec come from declared sources, checkboxes and A-exits criteria; the extractor’s only consumer is the read step. Figure 6A now classes that edge not realised rather than realised in code, not drawn.

Preference audit

The practical answer, as the evidence gives it. The machine encodes focus as a rule (one target per flight, a click that advances no want ends it) and as a weight in the class family’s cross-target choice — but the class weights are Joe’s ruling while the class assignment is an agent’s reading of corpus relation rows, so whether the thing being weighted is what he means by “necessary adjacent work” is not established.
