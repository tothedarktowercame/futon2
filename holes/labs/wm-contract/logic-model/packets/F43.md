F43: a retry child that died on another open injury re-queues without spending the retry budget (futon2 L, one file, one test)

Goal. The meta-injury ledger's re-queue budget (`:retry-limit` 3) bounds the retry children that ran and tested nothing. A child that died on a different open injury is evidence about that injury, not about this repair, so it re-queues without spending the budget. Today every `:retry-inconclusive` observation spends it. Live case: injury `injury/d755c886a22d6fc8c04db7fada64089e07157f62` in `data/wm-meta-injuries/ledger.edn` is `:enacted` at attempt 4 with `:requeues 2` (ordinal 22); its three retry children so far all died on other injuries (ordinal 18's pin is ordinal 17's `:cause-record-pin`, injury `821c15d0`; 21's is 20's, `75747f06`; 24's is 23's, `42fd2c21`). The next re-queue makes 3; the one after abandons `:retry-budget-spent` (`append-requeue`, `(>= requeues (:retry-limit ledger))` before the increment), with no `:port-receipt-pin`, so nothing can reopen it. That verdict would be on a patch no child has yet tested.

Where (read before editing): `src/futon2/aif/meta_injury_ledger.clj`. `append-requeue` (435-480): takes `ledger injury-id new-retry-click`, reads `(latest ledger injury-id)`, abandons at the limit, else appends `:enacted` with `:requeues (inc requeues)`. `pending-requeue` (397-409): the attributed `:retry-inconclusive` observation for the latest enacted retry click. The `:requeue/evidence` defect in the validator (183-196) requires `(:requeues entry)` = `(inc (:requeues previous))` and `≤ retry-limit`. The observation is written by `meta_terminal_injury.clj` 185 `append-other-injury-retry-observation` with `:record-pin` = the classification's pin, which is the other injury's `:cause-record-pin` on its `:open` entry; the no-work arm of `integrate` writes a `:retry-inconclusive` observation whose pin no injury names. So the ledger already holds the distinction; nothing new needs to be written by the terminal side.

Change (`meta_injury_ledger.clj` only). In `append-requeue`, after the three refusals: let `observation` be `(pending-requeue ledger injury-id)` and `other` be an entry of the ledger with `:state :open`, `:injury-id` not equal to this one, and `:cause-record-pin` equal to `(:record-pin observation)`. When `other` exists, append the `:enacted` re-queue entry with `:requeues` unchanged (not incremented), the same `:retry-click`, `:patch-sha`, `:reload-receipt`, `:integration-commit` handling as today, plus `:requeue-cause {:kind :other-open-injury :injury-id (:injury-id other) :record-pin (:record-pin observation)}`. The budget check (`>=` the limit, abandonment) applies only when no `other` exists; then the entry is as today, `:requeue-cause {:kind :no-injury}` optional. In the validator's `:requeue/evidence` clause: an entry whose `:requeue-cause` is `:other-open-injury` must have `:requeues` equal to the previous entry's and must name an injury id that has an `:open` entry earlier in the ledger whose `:cause-record-pin` equals the entry's `:requeue-cause :record-pin`, and that pin must equal the pending observation's pin (recompute from the entries, the validator is pure); any other entry keeps today's increment rule. Entries without `:requeue-cause` validate as today: no new required key. The production file `data/wm-meta-injuries/ledger.edn` must still read `:valid` through `validate-ledger` after the change; read it and report the status (read only, do not write it).
