Design (P `src/futon2/aif/meta_self_heal_production.clj` only; do not change the coordinator or the ledger):
- In `route!`, after `coordinator/coordinate` returns and its ledger and transaction have been persisted (the `lw`/`tw` writes at P:1114-1134 and the `settle` path), if the result is `{:status :held :reason :author-failed}` and the persisted ledger's active entry for that injury is `:reopened` (not `:abandoned`), then in the same call: retire the attempt-n transaction exactly as the resume block does (`retire-stale-transaction!`, P:792-807), `coordinator/prepare` attempt n+1 from the persisted durable ledger (reload it from `ledger-path` through `store/load-durable-ledger`, do not reuse the in-memory map), bind and coordinate again with the same `ports` and the same readiness/diagnosis/acceptance bindings (they belong to the injury, not the attempt), persist again, and loop. Stop when the result is anything other than a reopening author failure, or when the ledger's active entry is `:abandoned`. The attempt limit on the ledger bounds the loop; do not add a counter of your own.
- The final disposition is the last attempt's, plus `:continued-from`, a vector of `{:attempt n :reason :author-failed :transaction-retired {:path .. :attempt n}}` for each attempt the click moved past, in order. A click that did not continue has no `:continued-from` key (absent, not empty).
- A retirement refusal (`:meta-self-heal/transaction-retire-failed`) inside the loop is returned as the disposition, as the resume block does today.
- Each attempt's ledger write and transaction write happen before the next attempt starts, so a crash mid-loop leaves the same state the next click would have resumed from anyway.
