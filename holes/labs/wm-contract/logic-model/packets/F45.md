Goal. Cause: `coordinate-queued-retry` (`src/futon2/aif/meta_repair_coordinator.clj` 239-258) names the retry-port phase `(requeue-phase n)` with `n = (:requeues latest)` and mints the new click id at `(inc n)` before the append.
