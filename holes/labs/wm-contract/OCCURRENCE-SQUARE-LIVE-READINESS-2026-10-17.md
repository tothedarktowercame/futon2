# Occurrence-square live readiness — 2026-10-17

## Production sequence

1. The outer selector chooses and retains one target.
2. The existing selected-target phase publishes a validated reading if needed.
3. With `:require-occurrence-squares? true`, family construction stops before
   G when that reading has no square registry record.
4. `occurrence-square-authoring` sends the designated author the exact
   occurrence ids, cited fragments and the four written pattern parts.  The
   author supplies situation elements and evidence sources only.
5. Code constructs each `:wm/three-halves-square-v1`, validates its maps,
   pattern bytes, source SHA and written parts, publishes the sealed registry,
   then reruns inner construction once.  The outer selector is retained.
6. The pre-selector identity join binds every executable reading policy to its
   canonical cascade digest.  Pattern-grain graph retractions cannot become
   executable by borrowing citation evidence.
7. Selection reaches the already-installed held-cascade compiler, journal and
   D4 same-click repair protocol.

Authoring failure is thrown as an incomplete construction error at
`:occurrence-square-authoring`; it is not classified as an abstention,
refusal, or evidence that no policy exists.
