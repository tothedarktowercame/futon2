## 3. The instrument — CORRECTED 2026-09-04

> **Correction.** As first written, this section said three characters of Joe's
> words reached the embedder on the codex surface. That was wrong: three
> characters is what reached the **evidence record**. The embedding input is a
> different string and the store never sees it. Numbers below are corrected;
> the derivation is in `FINDINGS-embedding-pipeline.md` §1. What survives is
> sharper than what it replaces — the store does not record what was embedded,
> so the tagging layer cannot be audited from the store alone.

`dev.clj/context-retrieval!` builds

```clojure
proto-text (str (subs user-msg 0 200) " " (subs response-text 0 200))   ; embedded
"query"    (subs proto-text 0 (min 100 (count proto-text)))             ; recorded
```

So up to 401 characters are embedded and the first 100 of them are logged. Two
things follow for the census, one of them the reverse of what run 1 reported.

**Half the embedding input is the agent's reply, by design.** The comment calls
`proto-text` "the A->B protopattern": the tagged object is the exchange, not the
ask. Joe's 2026-09-04T06:12 dictation says the same thing from the other side —
*"it's all A, B exchanges where the A is the Operator."* So a tag that reads
like the reply is the instrument working as built, not a defect.

**The codex surface loses roughly half the operator's budget, not all of it.**
`extract-user-message` strips everything up to `"\nUser message:\n"`. The claude
surface emits that marker, so the surface-contract header is stripped and the
operator gets a full 200 characters. The codex bell envelope

```
--- CURRENT TURN ---
Surface: emacs-repl
From: joe
To: codex-7
Origin: operator
Caller: joe
---

```

carries no such marker, so all 97 characters of it stay in, and about 103
characters of Joe's words reach the embedder on those four turns instead of 200.
Real, and half the size of the effect run 1 claimed.

**What the store shows.** Because only the first 100 characters are recorded,
the logged `"query"` on those four turns is 97 characters of envelope plus
`the`, `So `, `I'm`, `Wel`. That is a logging artefact, and it is also why the
producer's stage-1 content join fails on exactly those four turns — the record
does not contain enough of the turn to match it. `03-coverage.edn`'s
`:instrument-fidelity` numbers measure the record on that basis and are labelled
accordingly.

what the limit did to this window.

All six disagree with the store. Five are **anti-causal** — the recorded ruling

today, so this is a defect in the record and not in a computation. The repair is
small and mechanical — take `:reviewed-at` from `:evidence/at` of the cited
turn — and it belongs to whoever owns the J rows, not to this excursion.
