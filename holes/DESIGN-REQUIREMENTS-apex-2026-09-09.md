# The four apex design requirements

Written down at Joe's direction (RULINGS-walkthrough-2026-09-09.md Item 26:
"we should take these design requirements, I write them down some place").
Source: the pinned Cascade Live apex thesis (snapshot 2026-09-06, sha
a93ec6fb...; pin at labs/wm-contract/runs/U88-cascade-live/), induced from
19 members of completed/observed work. Joe endorsed all four on 2026-09-09.

The apex they serve (verbatim from the pin): "Can a community of humans and
machines doing open-ended work leave an account of itself structured enough
that the work can steer itself -- so that activity becomes inference rather
than motion?"

## R-A. Records carry warrant

Ground (verbatim): "An assertion severed from its derivation cannot be
checked, learned from, or recovered."

Induced from 6 members: M-first-flights (records carry their derivations),
P4 (completion prose without execution evidence), PA11z (returns stored
trimmed: conclusions retained, warrant discarded), unqueryable trails,
undeclared cascades, leaf-5 (silent transport loss).

State 2026-09-09: one member has an executable satisfaction condition (the
first-flights replay: removing the measurement ground leaves the numeric
error identical and flips the calibration mask).

## R-B. One queryable self-account

Ground (verbatim): "Knowing what exists and its state, with provenance, at
stack scale."

Induced from 4 members: leaf-2 (four evidence vocabularies, one world), P2
(no provenance-bearing projection of stack state), P1 (self-observation
lags actuation), leaf-6-4-4 (unused infrastructure yields no fitness
evidence).

State 2026-09-09: prerequisite-satisfaction computed on the capability
graph for one completed subject; the unified issue board is work of this
kind, published.

## R-C. Feedback reaches every participant

Ground (verbatim): "The loop's human and stakeholder edges are where
learning silently dies."

Induced from 4 members: Box-12/PA11z (3 of 7 lifecycle stages are operator
turns), agent discoveries do not teach humans, UKRN trainers' equity,
leaf-cycle (understanding did not update the argument).

State 2026-09-09: the EXPLICIT ZERO — no carrier in channel C or seeded-C;
the nearest existing carrier scores "feedback delivered: false" the same as
delivery (substring containment; probe recorded in the E-C-realization
excursion). Caveat from the pin: project-domain problems join through this
requirement as instances.

## R-D. Accountable next-action choice, including goal formation

Ground (verbatim): "Deciding what to do next, accountably, under
uncertainty -- with the goals themselves formable, not given."

Induced from 5 members: WM core (G over policies; C; find/organise),
M-aif-policy-conditioned-eig, policy-grain compliance, R11 shared-budget
arbitration, C537 C_ser (pre-goal preference, serendipity).

State 2026-09-09: the densest carriers — G/C/find/organise all moved today
(find closed on an existence witness; C separated from scalar risk and
instantiated; fold and depth run-configurable). The outer-loop
task-selection requirement (E-pre-go-live brief) is this requirement's
goal-formation edge.

## What these are NOT (Item 26)

Not weights — "what are they weights of" is the open question, and Joe
rejected the weights framing as minimalistic. Whether each requirement is
best merged into the system as a preference (a C-family carrier), an
institution (rules-in-use over observation access, participation, and
roles — cf. Joe's IAD/Ostrom strand, corneli2016), a design pattern (an
if/however/then in the library with correspondence controls), or several
of these at different grains, is the subject of the excursion this
document seeds (E-requirements-integration). The one adjacent settled
precedent: the 2026-09-09 separation ruling, where two candidate "values"
turned out to be different KINDS of object (a preference distribution and
a typed scalar risk contribution) that must not be merged.

Standing evidence limit carried from the pin: the live arrow graph is
single-flavour (176 arrows, all :close-hole) — delta-g is currently
evidence about the graph's expressiveness, not about preferences.
