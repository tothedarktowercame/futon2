**solved** (checked before the machine runs again):
1. **The registry replaces the fixture, and it is derived.** `GroundingRegistry := Mission → Option Witness`
   where membership is *derived* from a declared grounding relation (a mission is groundable iff a CLean path
   with a recorded producer exists for it — the relation is data, the set is computed), and a mission outside
   it yields the **typed absence** `:no-grounding-for-mission`, never `nil`/`0` (GainChain's `typedAbsence`).
   **Falsifier:** `bayesian-structure-learning` is outside today; the checker must return the typed absence for
   it, and a fifth mission added to the relation must be groundable *without editing a map* (I1 gets its
   refusing and accepting witnesses).
2. **An enactment is an attempt and its typed record.** `Enactment := {mission, cascade, result}` where `result`
   is the three-arm union the executor actually returns: `:constructed-wiring` (the fold engine built a wiring
   map), `:no-construction` carrying the reason it did not (`:engine-returned-no-wiring` at `enact.clj:147`,
   `:empty-cascade` at `enact.clj:262`), or `:enactment-failed` carrying phase, exception class and message
   (`enact.clj:148-160`), persisted onto the tick at `enact.clj:283-292`. `R16→R2`'s payload IS that typed
   result, and the edge observes a CONSTRUCTION — an in-model object — not an outward act
   (`control-map-edges.edn:23-55`, whose `:payload-pins` at `:42-52` pin each arm to its emitting line).
   **Falsifier:** an enactment that reports an untyped `nil`, or a tick where the executor threw that is
   byte-identical to a tick where nothing was attempted, must be refused: the absence has to name its reason.

   — `act` iff both present ∧ `S_cascade > 0` ∧ `ΔS_coverage < 0`; `abstain` if either absent; never F or G.
   **Falsifier:** the glossary's own example (`+1.2`, `−0.8` passes; a positive coverage delta fails).

**TRIPWIRE — standing, from the 2026-09-03 ruling (DECISIONS-PENDING #2, futon2 `101d609`).**
The external-witness obligation clause 2 used to carry is not discharged; it is *armed to re-open*. Clause 2 as
re-scoped is scoped to the action vocabulary the machine has today, and every member of that vocabulary is
**self-witnessing**: a commit, a board write, a bell — the record IS the external state, so an independent
re-observation would read back exactly what the act already wrote, and the strong promise had become an unbuilt
claim rather than a safeguard.

**The self-witnessing test**, to be applied to a proposed action type before it is armed: *does the act's
success differ from its record?*
- **No** — a commit is in the object store or it is not; the record and the effect are the same object.
  Self-witnessing; clause 2 as re-scoped covers it and no external witness is owed.
- **Yes** — a mail the relay accepts and never delivers; a deploy that returns 200 and rolls back; a payment
  authorised and then reversed. "I tried" and "it happened" are different facts, and only the first is
  in the machine's own record.

**Any new action type that fails the test re-opens the external-witness obligation BEFORE that action type may
be armed** — the independent read-back and its binding into the next observation must exist first. This is a
gate on *arming*, not on building or on discussing, and it is what allowed clause 2 to be narrowed without
narrowing the safety claim: the obligation now sits exactly where the safety concern is real.
`C78-outward-act-refusal.md` stays exactly as it is. Its refusal to build an unauthored outward actuator is
what keeps this re-scope honest — it names the falsifier, and the census found nothing satisfying it.
