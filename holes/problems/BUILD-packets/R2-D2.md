## The trap this packet exists to avoid — read it as an acceptance clause
`Channel` must be read from the **source declaration** `observation-channels`. It must **not** be
derived from the trace — not as a union of observed keys, not as the modal key set, not "the keys of
record 1". A `Channel` derived from the corpus makes `∀ tick, keys tick.observation = Channel` true by
construction and turns the whole checker into the data certifying itself, which is the self-certification
R9 exists to refuse and the reason this record's problem statement says *"the vector's key set is a type,
not a docstring."*

**The discriminating test, which your fixture must contain:** a synthetic trace record carrying a
**fifteenth, undeclared key** must **fire**. A checker whose `Channel` came from the trace cannot fire on
it — it would simply widen. That test is the one thing here that distinguishes a real checker from a
tautological one, so it is not optional.
