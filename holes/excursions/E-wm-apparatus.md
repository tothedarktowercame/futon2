## 1. What is already covered, and what is not

The WM's existing assurance work is about the *domain*: the R-node dossiers
tie each control-map vertex to its equations and checkers
(p4ng/sec-rnode-dossiers-generated.tex); the wm-nouns / wm-verbs /
wm-organization / wm-evidence lanes closed 144 ledger cases against that
map (p4ng/sec-workflow-generated.tex); the Lean corpus now carries all 18
registry equations (U68, 2026-09-08, `machinePolicySet` the last). That
perimeter answers: *is the mathematics stated, transcribed, witnessed?*

The APM campaign showed what that perimeter does not answer. Its own
end-to-end analysis (futon3c/holes/T-apm-recurring-failure-end-to-end.md)
found every defect OUTSIDE the green Lean perimeter: 111 of 126 decided
frame parks were apparatus faults — recording, delivery, liveness,
identity — while the mathematics passed. The WM runs on the same kind of
apparatus: a build loop, seats, gates, registries, an Agency transport.
The assurances this excursion names are about *that* layer: the claim we
want to be able to make is not "the equations are right" but "when the
machine runs, what it records is what happened, and when it breaks, we
find out from the apparatus rather than from Joe."

The instrument for the claim is the assurance frontier agreed in
C583-assurance-frontier-agreement.md: for every pattern × violation-
signature pair, exactly one of {live mechanism with a witnessed detector,
ticket in the DAG (:U70), recorded non-applicability} — and a lint that
makes the fourth state (silent omission) impossible to hold quietly.

## 2. The patterns, read against the WM specifically

mechanism; frontier entries still owe sha-pinned witnesses):

- `one-authority-per-question` — the registries are single-reader by
  construction (aif-equations :choices for rulings, worklist for tickets);

  affirmative achievement answers; the rebind probe refused to re-bind

- `the-system-stops-on-schedule` — the loop stops itself when the board

## 3. The assurances to build, in dependency order

1. **The DAG itself** (:U70) — tickets with :assures edges at
   pattern × signature grain, per the C583 schema. Everything below hangs
   off it.
2. **The two frontier fences** (:U70 acceptance) — the no-fourth-state
   lint and the witness-staleness comparator, both commissioned by
   induced failure before the render is trusted. A monitor of assurance
   is still a monitor; the APM trial watched three uncommissioned
   monitors lie in one day.
3. **Detector commissioning** (:U73) and **the standing caller
   comparator** (:U74) — the two scorecard gaps with mechanisms already
   sketched.
4. **Receipt discipline** (:U71, :U72) — the pin that moves with the
   population, at source, plus the drain.
5. **Enumeration pinning and the vocabulary seam** — Lean-side coupling
   tickets to be minted inside :U70's sweep (they need statement-hashes
   and owners, which the DAG build supplies).

What this buys, stated as the falsifiable claim the frontier renders: at
any commit, every apparatus-pattern signature is either mechanically
guarded (and the guard has been seen to fire), or in the workplan with its
dependencies drawn, or recorded as not applying — and a reader can tell
which, without asking anyone.
